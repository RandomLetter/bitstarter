    How it works

    • Each round, plantation tiles are auctioned; the lowest bidder becomes the Canal Overseer.
    • Players place their tiles and then bribe the Overseer to build an irrigation canal where it helps them.
    • Plantations not next to a canal dry out; deserts are worth nothing.
    • After all tiles are placed (11 rounds for 3–4 players, 9 for 5) the connected, irrigated plantation areas pay out; the richest landowner wins.
